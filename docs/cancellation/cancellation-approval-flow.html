From Cancel to Closed

PRC_APP_ENQ · central approval queue

PRC_APP_ENQ belongs to the core banking's general approval module, which gathers every record waiting for approval. Every new or changed record goes through it when it is submitted, and again when it is approved. It doesn't post anything itself: the end-of-day and start-of-day procedures carry out the actual transactions. The table shows what each call passes; amber marks the values that differ.
