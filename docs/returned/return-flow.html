Collateral Management · Returned (new module)

Return and Resubmit

The approval screens in Oracle Forms can only approve. The new design gives the approver three decisions: Authorize, Return for correction, or Dismiss for good. A returned request goes to the submitter's Returned queue, reopens on its own entry screen, and comes back for approval with the same collateral number.

Applies to: creation, amendment and cancellation requestsStatus: proposed design, as built in the prototype

Return and resubmit flow

Three lanes: what the approver does, what the submitter does, and what the system records. The dashed line is the loop back to the approver.

What reopens

A returned request opens on the screen it was entered on, filled in, with an amber banner showing who returned it, when, and the reason.

RequestScreenFilled in withOn resubmit
CreationCreationCustomer and collateral type selected, every field and document as submittedSame collateral number; replaces the pending row
AmendmentAmendment detailsThe proposed changes, highlighted against the approved valuesSame collateral number; the new changes replace the returned ones
CancellationCancellation detailsThe read-only collateral and the closure reasonSame collateral number and reason, unless changed
